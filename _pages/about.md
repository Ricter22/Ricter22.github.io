I am a PhD researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Information Retrieval at the [University of Southern Denmark](https://portal.findresearcher.sdu.dk/en/persons/rite/), where I am part of the SDU Center for Industrial Software at the Maersk Mc-Kinney Moller Institute.

My research focuses on dataset discovery, retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, large language models, and software architecture. I am particularly interested in making data easier to find while keeping AI-generated results faithful, transparent, and auditable.

Before beginning my PhD, I earned an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Politecnico di Milano and a BSc in Computer and Automation Engineering from Marche Polytechnic University. I also worked as a Software and DevOps Engineer at Blue Reply and gained international experience in Germany and Denmark.

Alongside research, I teach artificial intelligence, supervise student projects, and mentor aspiring data scientists.

Research interests
======

- Information retrieval and dataset discovery
- Retrieval-augmented generation and agentic systems
- Knowledge graphs and data spaces
- Large language models and trustworthy AI
- Software architecture and AI-assisted development
