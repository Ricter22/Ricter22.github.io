This is a static academic website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not enable analytics, advertising, or a comment service.

## Browser preferences

The theme switch stores your light or dark preference in your browser's local storage. This preference stays in your browser and is used when you return to the site.

## Hosting and external resources

Your browser contacts GitHub Pages to load this website. Some resources, including academic icons, are loaded from jsDelivr. Those providers receive the information your browser sends with a request, such as your IP address. Pages containing mathematical notation may also load MathJax from jsDelivr.

Links to external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Contact me at [rite@mmmi.sdu.dk](mailto:rite@mmmi.sdu.dk) with questions about this website.
